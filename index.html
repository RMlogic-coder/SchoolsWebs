About Our School

Welcome to Pathways Global School

Founded in 2014, Pathways Global School has been dedicated to providing quality education with modern infrastructure, caring teachers, and a holistic approach to learning.

Located at Dharamkot Road, Kot Ise Khan (District Moga, Punjab), our campus offers an inspiring, safe, and disciplined environment where children excel academically, morally, and physically under the prestigious ICSE Board (Affiliation No. PU-131).
