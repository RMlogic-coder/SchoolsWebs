Our Wings

Nurturing Growth At Every Stage

At Pathways Global School, our curriculum under the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (ICSE) offers an age-appropriate balance of academic depth, scientific inquiry, language mastery, and creative expression.

Early Childhood

Pre-Primary Wing (Playway, Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Our early childhood program emphasizes learning through discovery, storytelling, music, and interactive play. We cultivate curiosity in a safe, affectionate atmosphere where young minds develop fine motor skills, social empathy, and early phonetics.

  • Playway & Montessori blended pedagogical approach
  • Rich visual and manipulative learning toys
  • Foundational communication and phonetic awareness
  • Air-conditioned, colorful, child-safe classrooms
Foundation Years

Primary Wing (Classes I to V)

During the primary years, we build solid conceptual foundations in languages, mathematics, and environmental science. Smart digital boards make abstract ideas vivid and memorable, sparking a love for reading and exploration.

  • Strong command of English, Hindi, and Punjabi
  • Concept-driven mathematics and mental calculations
  • Hands-on Environmental Studies and nature projects
  • Weekly library, arts, and physical education periods
Discovery & Inquiry

Middle School Wing (Classes VI to VIII)

Middle school transitions students from general study into specialized scientific disciplines—Physics, Chemistry, Biology—alongside advanced Humanities and Computer Coding. Practical lab work develops scientific temperament.

  • Experimental learning in dedicated science labs
  • Computer applications and coding curriculum
  • Analytical thinking in Mathematics & Geography
  • Inter-house debates, quizzes, and sporting tournaments
ICSE Board Excellence

Secondary Wing (Classes IX & X - ICSE)

Our Secondary Wing prepares students for the rigorous Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) examinations. We combine intensive syllabus coverage, regular assessments, individual doubt-clearing, and career counseling.

  • Full compliance with Council (ICSE) New Delhi syllabus
  • Extensive laboratory practicals and project submissions
  • Mock tests, remedial classes, and personalized feedback
  • Guidance for competitive entrance and future career paths
